# Team Working Agreement

## Branch Naming

Use:

`feature/name-short-desc`

Example:

`feature/henry-auth`

Do not push development work directly to `main`.

## Commit Messages

Use:

`[PB-XX] Short description`

Example:

`[PB-01] Add citizen login`

## Daily Check-in

Post in the team GC by 8 PM:

- What I finished
- What I will work on next
- Blockers

## Meeting Cadence

- Sprint Planning: Day 1
- Mid-Sprint Check: Day 7
- Sprint Review + Retrospective: Day 13 or 14

## Definition of Done

A task follows the team's Definition of Done documented in:

`docs/definition-of-done.md`
